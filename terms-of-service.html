What Clip Maker is

Clip Maker is a desktop program that cuts short vertical clips from stream recordings and can upload them to your own social media accounts (such as TikTok) using those platforms' official APIs.

Your content and permissions

  • You are responsible for the clips you create and post, including following the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines and the rules of any other platform you post to.
  • Clip Maker never posts publicly on its own: clips sent to TikTok go to your TikTok inbox, and you review and post them yourself in the TikTok app.
