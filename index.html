Clip Maker

Clip Maker is a Windows desktop tool for streamers and clippers. It finds the best moments in a stream, cuts them into vertical clips with subtitles, and sends them to the creator's own TikTok inbox so they can review and post them.

How it works

  • Load a stream you own or have permission to use.
  • Clip Maker picks the highlights and makes 9:16 clips with subtitles and a suggested caption.
  • Connect your own TikTok account and send a clip to your TikTok inbox. You finish and post it in the TikTok app.

Everything runs on your own computer. Clip Maker has no server and does not collect your data. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
